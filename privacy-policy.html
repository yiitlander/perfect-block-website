1. About This Privacy Policy

Welcome to Perfect Block (the “Game” or the “Application”), developed and published by KAA Digital.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, access, or play Perfect Block on Android or iOS devices.

We aim to keep the Game simple and privacy-conscious. Perfect Block does not require you to create an account, and the Game’s core gameplay data is stored locally on your device.

Application: Perfect Block

3. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

KAA Digital does not operate an account or user registration system. The Game does not directly ask you to provide or submit:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Passwords or account credentials
  • Financial or payment information
  • Social media account information
  • Camera or microphone recordings
  • Contacts or calendar information
  • Precise GPS location information

4. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Perfect Block stores certain game-related information locally on your device using Hive, a local storage/database technology.

This may include:

  • Unlocked levels and game progress
  • Stars or other gameplay progress
  • Game preferences such as sound and vibration settings
  • Language preferences
  • Hint-related local game state
  • Other information required to preserve your gameplay experience
Important: This gameplay information is intended to remain on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to servers operated by KAA Digital. KAA Digital does not maintain a server-side player account or cloud profile for this gameplay data.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Perfect Block is supported by in-game advertising and uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertisements.

Ad Formats Used in Perfect Block

  • Rewarded advertisements: You may voluntarily choose to watch a rewarded video for an in-game benefit, such as a hint or an Arcade continuation.
  • Interstitial advertisements: These may be shown at designated transition points in the Game.

Rewarded advertisements are presented as optional user-initiated actions. Interstitial advertisements are not intended to appear unexpectedly during active puzzle interaction.

Information Processed by AdMob

When advertising functionality is used and the device is connected to the internet, Google AdMob may process certain device, technical, advertising, and interaction information for purposes such as delivering advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing invalid activity, and supporting the operation of its advertising services.

Depending on your device, operating system, settings, consent status, and applicable laws, this may include:

  • IP address and information that may be used to estimate general location
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • Information relating to ad impressions, clicks, and interactions
  • Technical and diagnostic information related to advertising delivery
  • Other information processed by Google as described in its applicable privacy documentation

Google processes this information independently from KAA Digital in accordance with Google's applicable policies.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google Uses Information From Sites or Apps That Use Its Services: Google partner-site information

Where required by applicable law, Perfect Block may present privacy and advertising consent choices before requesting advertisements.

These choices may include options relating to personalized advertising, non-personalized advertising, and the use of information by Google and its advertising partners.

Where required, Perfect Block uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide and manage applicable privacy choices.

Users may also be provided with an in-app privacy options entry point allowing them to review or change applicable privacy choices.

The availability and content of these choices may vary depending on your location, device, applicable law, and Google's current consent requirements.

7. Tracking and Advertising Identifiers

The current version of Perfect Block does not directly implement Apple's App Tracking Transparency authorization flow and does not directly access the iOS IDFA from the Game's own code.

Perfect Block does not create user accounts or maintain a user profile for the purpose of cross-app identification.

Advertising identifiers and other advertising-related information may nevertheless be processed by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob in accordance with their applicable policies, device settings, and consent requirements.

8. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Perfect Block or by its third-party advertising provider may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and preserving core gameplay functionality
  • Saving game progress and preferences locally
  • Providing optional rewarded advertisements
  • Delivering and measuring advertisements
  • Preventing invalid advertising activity and fraud
  • Maintaining and supporting the technical operation of advertising services
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

KAA Digital does not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

9. Third-Party Services

The primary third-party service integrated into Perfect Block for online functionality is Google AdMob.

Perfect Block does not currently use:

  • Firebase
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Cloud Firestore
  • User account or authentication services
  • Third-party analytics platforms
  • Third-party attribution platforms

Google's processing of information through AdMob is governed by Google's own policies.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Game Data

Because gameplay information is stored locally on your device, KAA Digital does not maintain a server-side copy of your game progress.

Depending on your device and operating system, locally stored application data may be removed by:

  • Using the operating system's available option to clear the application's stored data, or
  • Uninstalling the Game.

The exact behavior of local-data deletion is determined by the operating system and device.

Advertising Data

KAA Digital does not control the retention periods applicable to information processed by Google AdMob. Information processed by Google is handled according to Google's applicable policies, legal obligations, and retention practices.

11. Children's Privacy

Perfect Block is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children.

The Game does not include account registration, chat, messaging, or social features designed to encourage users to submit personal information.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the Game.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted to a third-party service in connection with the Game, please contact us at yigit.toroslu@kaa.digital.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Game.

  • Gameplay information stored by Perfect Block is kept within the application's local storage environment provided by the device operating system.
  • Advertising-related network communication is handled by Google AdMob using security measures implemented by Google.

No security measure can guarantee absolute security, and the security practices of third-party services are governed by their own policies.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information processed about you, including rights such as:

  • Accessing information
  • Requesting correction
  • Requesting deletion
  • Restricting or objecting to certain processing
  • Managing applicable advertising or consent preferences
  • Opting out of certain uses where applicable

Because KAA Digital does not maintain user accounts or a server-side database containing individual player profiles, we may not be able to identify or retrieve locally stored gameplay information associated with a particular person.

For information processed directly by Google AdMob, you should also review Google's privacy tools and policies.

14. Offline Gameplay

The core gameplay of Perfect Block does not require an active internet connection.

You can play the core puzzle experience offline.

Internet connectivity is required for online advertising functionality, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

When the device is offline, advertisements may not be available.
